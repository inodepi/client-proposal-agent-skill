# Worked Example

A fictional proposal built from a thin brief, a rate card, and one constraint. Client: **Ironwood Strength**, a boutique gym relaunching its site.

## Input

**Brief:** "We need a new site before the January membership push. People should see the class schedule and book a spot. We want to sell memberships online too. Brand exists, photos are mostly there, one person (the owner) will manage it."

**Rate card:** $110/hour; fixed packages preferred. 12-week hard deadline.

**Constraints:** one approver, existing brand, Stripe for payments, no in-house developer.

## Proposal excerpt

### Summary

Ironwood needs a site that turns January interest into booked classes and paid memberships, not a brochure. We will build an eight-page site on your existing brand, connect the class schedule to online booking, and take membership payments through Stripe, so the owner manages everything from one dashboard. The goal is a launch-ready site by week 10, with two weeks of buffer before the January push.

### What success looks like

- A visitor can find a class and book it in under three taps
- New members can buy a membership without calling the gym
- The owner can publish a schedule change without a developer

Constraints: January launch, one approver, Stripe account required.

### Scope

**Included**

- 8 pages: home, schedule, classes, memberships, coaches, about, contact, policies
- Class schedule page connected to the existing booking provider
- Stripe checkout for three membership tiers
- CMS setup so the schedule and class descriptions are editable
- Two revision rounds per page group
- Launch support for two weeks

**Not included**

- Photography or video production
- Copywriting beyond light editing of supplied text
- Email marketing flows or SMS
- Ongoing site maintenance after the support window

**Client responsibilities**

- Brand assets and final copy by week 2
- Stripe account created and verified by week 3
- One named approver for feedback
- Feedback within three business days per round

### Timeline

| Phase | Work | Duration | Weeks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Discovery, sitemap, wireframes | 1.5 weeks | 1–2 |
| 2 | Visual design, 2 rounds | 2 weeks | 3–4 |
| 3 | Build, CMS, integrations | 3 weeks | 5–7 |
| 4 | Content entry, QA, booking tests | 2 weeks | 8–9 |
| 5 | Launch, buffer, training | 1 week | 10 |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5-page site | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Class schedule embed | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Online booking | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Stripe memberships | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| CMS + owner training | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Launch support | 2 weeks | 2 weeks | 3 months |
| Priority timeline (10 weeks) | — | — | ✓ |
| Photography direction | — | — | ✓ |
| **Price** | **$6,500** | **$9,800** | **$14,500** |

Payment: 40% on signature, 30% at build start, 30% on delivery.

Recommended: the online booking and Stripe checkout are the January revenue, and the CMS keeps the schedule accurate without a developer.

### Terms

- Two revision rounds per page group; further rounds at $110/hour
- IP transfers on final payment
- Either party may terminate with 14 days' notice; work performed is payable
- Proposal valid until 30 November 2026

### Next step

Sign the agreement and pay the deposit by 28 November to hold the January slot.

## Internal notes — remove before sending

| Likely objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "It's more than we budgeted" | Essential at $6,500 covers the schedule and brand refresh; booking and memberships are what convert January traffic, so they are the first upgrade if budget opens. |
| "Can you launch before January?" | Premium adds a 10-week priority timeline; anything faster cuts QA time, which is where booking bugs hide. |
| "We could do this ourselves with a template" | Possible for the pages; the booking-to-schedule integration and Stripe setup are where template builds usually stall in January. |
