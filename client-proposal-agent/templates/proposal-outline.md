# Proposal Outline

Section skeleton with word budgets for a 2–5 page proposal. Delete a section rather than pad it.

```text
# {{Project name}} — Proposal for {{Client}}
Prepared by {{sender_name}} · {{date}} · Valid until {{valid_until}}

## 1. Summary
{{Three to five sentences: their problem, your approach, the outcome. No adjectives for their own sake.}}

## 2. What success looks like
- {{Measurable outcome 1}}
- {{Measurable outcome 2}}
Constraints: {{deadline, budget range, team, systems}}

## 3. Approach
{{The shape of the work in two short paragraphs: phases, why this sequence, what you need from them.}}

## 4. Scope
Included:
- {{Deliverable with acceptance criteria}}
- {{Deliverable with acceptance criteria}}

Not included:
- {{Exclusion the client might assume}}
- {{Exclusion the client might assume}}

Client responsibilities:
- {{Assets, access, approvals, feedback windows}}

## 5. Timeline
| Phase | Work | Duration | Dates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 6. Investment
| | Essential | Recommended | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| {{Core deliverable}} | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| {{Extra 1}} | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| {{Extra 2}} | — | — | ✓ |
| Timeline | {{weeks}} | {{weeks}} | {{weeks}} |
| **Price** | **{{price}}** | **{{price}}** | **{{price}}** |

Payment: {{deposit}}% on signature, {{milestones}}, final on delivery.

## 7. Assumptions
- {{Assumption that, if false, changes the price or date}}

## 8. Terms
- Two revision rounds per deliverable; further rounds at {{rate}}/hour
- IP transfers on full payment
- Either party may terminate with {{notice}} days' notice; work performed is payable
- Proposal valid until {{valid_until}}

## 9. Next step
{{One action}} by {{date}}.
```
